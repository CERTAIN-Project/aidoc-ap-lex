Introduction back to ToC

The AIDOC-AP LEX Extension adds to the AIDOC-AP core what is needed to document how an AI system meets legal and ethical requirements: which management systems, assessments, measures and documents an organisation has in place, which risks and incidents they address, and which persons and authorities are involved. The core describes the technical documentation required by Annex IV of the EU AI Act. The extension covers the obligations of providers and deployers in Articles 9 to 17 (risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and cybersecurity, provider obligations, quality management) and Articles 72 and 73 (post-market monitoring, serious incidents), and the ethical requirements of the Assessment List for Trustworthy AI (ALTAI), the OECD AI Principles and the gender requirements of CERTAIN deliverable D3.2. It imports the core without changing any of its definitions. Terms of DPV, the DPV AI Act extension and VAIR are reused where they define the same concept; other correspondences are published as SKOS mappings.

Scope and method

The requirements were analysed from the wording of the AI Act in the AI Act Service Desk of the European Commission at the time of the analysis. Only obligations of providers and deployers were analysed; sections on market surveillance authorities and other public bodies were left out. The ethical competency questions follow ALTAI, the operational successor of the assessment list in the Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI, the OECD AI Principles, and the gender requirements of D3.2, Ethical considerations of AI compliance (Gender Audit Tool, GIA and SGIA method, QETAM framework, gender inclusivity checklist, ethical and gender risk register). Each requirement lists competency questions, the ontology terms needed to answer them and the evidence an organisation is expected to keep. Terms proposed in the analysis were merged where they overlapped and mapped to existing AIDOC-AP, AIRO or DPV terms where these exist; the mapping of every proposed term is published in the repository (term-mapping.csv).
